# 🌱 Aeterna – Digital Platform for Verified Environmental Impact

Aeterna is a multi-sided digital platform designed to improve the allocation, verification, and long-term monitoring of environmental projects.

🌐 **[View the Interactive Prototype](https://elfersi.github.io/aeterna-digital-platform/)**

## 📌 Project Overview

Aeterna connects environmental project developers, corporate buyers, independent verifiers, and long-term monitoring partners within a single digital ecosystem.

The platform aims to improve trust, transparency, and incentive alignment in environmental impact allocation.

## ⚙️ How It Works

1. **Project Submission** – Environmental project developers submit their projects.
2. **Independent Verification** – Projects are reviewed to assess their credibility and environmental claims.
3. **Permanence Scoring** – Eligible projects are evaluated based on environmental impact, biodiversity co-benefits, permanence probability, and cost efficiency.
4. **Vickrey Auction** – Verified projects enter a second-price sealed-bid auction mechanism.
5. **Escrow Mechanism** – Funds are managed through escrow and released according to predefined conditions.
6. **Long-Term Monitoring** – Environmental performance is monitored over time.

## ✨ Key Features

- Independent environmental project verification
- Permanence-based project scoring
- Vickrey second-price auction mechanism
- Escrow-based transactions
- Long-term environmental impact monitoring
- Multi-sided platform architecture
